Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: Expirio: Expiry & Reminders (com.arashabd.reminder)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and data privacy regulations, Expirio: Expiry & Reminders provides users with full control to permanently delete their account and associated cloud data.

Option 1: Instant In-App Account & Data Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the app installed on your Android device, you can instantly delete your account and all cloud data without waiting:

  1. Open Expirio: Expiry & Reminders on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile & Settings icon (gear icon in the top right menu).
  3. Ensure you are signed in to your Google Account.
  4. Tap the red button labeled "Delete Account & Data".
  5. Confirm the prompt. All your Firebase Firestore cloud backups and Google account links will be permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Email Data Deletion Request (For Users Without the App)

If you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to your Android device, you can submit a manual deletion request:

  • Send an Email To: arash.abdorrahimi.dev@gmail.com
  • Email Subject: Expirio Data Deletion Request
  • Include in Email Body: Your Google Account Email Address used for Expirio cloud backup.

Processing Time: Manual email requests are verified and permanently purged from Firebase Firestore within 7 business days.

What Types of Data Are Deleted?

  • Google Account Link: Firebase Authentication user record and OAuth credentials.
  • Cloud Reminders & Backups: All task, prescription, and perishable expiration records stored in Firebase Firestore under users/{uid}/.
  • Local Data: All local database records on your device are wiped upon in-app account deletion.

Data Retention

Once deleted via Option 1 or Option 2, your data cannot be recovered. We do not retain backup copies of deleted user accounts.
