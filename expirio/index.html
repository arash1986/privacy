Privacy Policy for Expirio: Expiry & Reminders

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 | Application Package: com.arashabd.reminder

Welcome to Expirio: Expiry & Reminders ("Expirio", "we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure experience when using our mobile application on Android.

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Account Data (Optional Google Sign-In)

Expirio allows usage in Guest Mode (100% offline, no account required) or via Google Account Sign-In.

  • Data Collected: When signing in with Google, we process your Display Name, Email Address, and Profile Picture URL via Firebase Authentication.
  • Purpose: Authenticating your identity and enabling cloud backup across devices.

B. Device Camera & Scanner Access

  • Permission: android.permission.CAMERA
  • Purpose: Used exclusively when you initiate optical barcode scanning (medical/food expiration) or receipt OCR processing via Google ML Kit.
  • Privacy Guarantee: Camera frames are processed on-device in real-time. No video feeds or photos are stored or transmitted.

C. System Alarms & Notifications

To deliver timely reminders and alarms, Expirio uses system permissions including POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT, and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED to reschedule alarms after device reboot.

D. AI Recipe Assistant (Google Gemini AI)

Expirio offers an AI Recipe Assistant powered by Google Gemini API. When generating recipes, the app sends only selected expiring food item names (e.g. "Milk, Eggs, Tomatoes") and language settings. No personal account credentials or device IDs are sent to AI servers.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

Expirio integrates trusted Google services:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Crashlytics & Analytics
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition
  • Google Gemini AI

3. Data Storage & Security

Security Architecture: Your data is stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database (Room). Cloud data is stored in Firebase Firestore (encrypted at rest and in transit). App configuration tokens are protected using native binary encryption and Google Cloud security restrictions.

4. User Controls & Data Deletion

You can delete local data anytime via App Settings or request cloud data deletion by emailing arash.abdorrahimi.dev@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

Expirio is not directed to children under 13 years of age.
